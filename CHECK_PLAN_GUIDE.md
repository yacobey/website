# How to Check Your Current Replit Plan

## Quick Check Methods

### Method 1: Replit Dashboard
1. Go to your Replit dashboard
2. Click your profile picture (top right corner)
3. Select "Account" or "Settings"
4. Look for "Plan" or "Subscription" section
5. You'll see one of these:
   - **Starter** (Free) - No custom domains
   - **Core** ($20-25/month) - Includes custom domains
   - **Teams** ($35-40/user/month) - Business plan

### Method 2: Deploy Button Test
1. Look at the top toolbar in your Replit project
2. If you see a "Deploy" button: You likely have Core or Teams
3. If no "Deploy" button: You're on Starter (Free)

### Method 3: Project Settings
1. In your Replit project, click the project name
2. Go to "Settings" tab
3. Look for "Deployments" or "Custom Domain" options
4. If missing: You're on Starter plan

## What Each Plan Includes

### Starter (Free)
- Development only
- No deployments
- No custom domains
- 10 public projects max

### Core ($20/month annually)
- Unlimited deployments
- Custom domain support
- $25 monthly usage credits
- Professional features

### Teams ($35/user/month annually)
- Everything in Core
- Team collaboration
- $40 monthly credits
- Business features

## Current Status Indicators

### Signs You're on Starter (Free):
- No "Deploy" button visible
- Can't access deployment settings
- Limited to development environment only

### Signs You're on Core/Teams:
- "Deploy" button in toolbar
- Can access deployment settings
- Custom domain options available

## Next Steps Based on Your Plan

### If You're on Starter:
1. Upgrade to Core plan ($20/month)
2. Deploy your website
3. Connect selamcpa.com domain

### If You're on Core/Teams:
1. Click "Deploy" to deploy your website
2. Connect selamcpa.com via deployment settings
3. Update DNS records

## Quick Verification
The easiest way: Look for the "Deploy" button in your project toolbar right now. If you don't see it, you need to upgrade to Core plan.
