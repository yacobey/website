| 6 | Value post from the guide: the $124 store-registration breakdown as a standalone post | Reach (numbers travel well) |

**Cadence rules (from the accounts this format works for):**
- 1–2 posts/day, at least one pure-value post for every ask.
- Links only in the final post of a thread or in bio — never in the hook.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; Threads' ranking rewards early conversation.
- Every Friday, repost the guide link with that week's progress numbers.
