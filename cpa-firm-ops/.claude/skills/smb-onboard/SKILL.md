# SMB Onboard

A structured 2-week onboarding for new CPA firm clients. Covers data access, baseline, and quick wins.

## Inputs to confirm
- Client name + entity type
- Services scope (bookkeeping, tax, advisory, payroll)
- Prior CPA? (transition vs net-new)
- Books current or behind? (catch-up?)

## Steps — Week 1: Access & Baseline
1. **Access** (collect & verify):
   - QBO accountant invite accepted
   - Bank read-only access (or statement upload SOP)
   - Payment processor logins (Stripe, Square, etc.)
   - Payroll access (Gusto, ADP, etc.)
   - Document portal access (encrypted)
2. **Baseline pull** (`/business-pulse`):
   - Snapshot of current financials
   - Open AR / AP positions
   - Cash runway estimate
3. **Discovery call notes**:
   - Top 3 things they want help with
   - Pain points with prior CPA
   - Owner's communication preference (Slack, email, weekly call, etc.)

## Steps — Week 2: Cleanup & Quick Wins
1. Books hygiene check: uncategorized transactions, undeposited funds, mis-mapped accounts.
2. Run `/month-end-prep` for the most recent closed month.
3. Find one quick win: a real tax saving, a process to automate, an invoice to recover.
4. Draft the **engagement summary doc**: scope, deliverables, cadence, fees.

## Output
- Onboarding checklist (✅ status per item)
- Baseline financials snapshot
- "Quick win" memo
- Engagement summary doc
- 30/60/90 plan for client
