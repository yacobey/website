# URGENT SSL FIX - Simple Steps for Non-Technical Users

## What's Wrong
Your website shows "Not secure" because the SSL certificate was created for the wrong domain name. It needs to be recreated specifically for selamcpa.com.

## Simple Fix (Takes 5 minutes + waiting time)

### Step 1: Go to Your Replit Dashboard
1. Open a new browser tab
2. Go to: https://replit.com/deployments
3. Find your website deployment
4. Click on it to open

### Step 2: Remove Your Domain (Temporarily)
1. Look for "Custom Domains" section
2. Find "selamcpa.com" in the list
3. Click the "Remove" or "Delete" button next to it
4. Confirm removal when asked
5. **Wait 15 minutes** - Set a timer! This is important.

### Step 3: Add Your Domain Back
1. After 15 minutes, go back to the same page
2. Look for "Add Custom Domain" button
3. Type: selamcpa.com
4. **Make sure "Enable SSL" is checked**
5. Click "Add Domain"

### Step 4: Wait for SSL Certificate
- This takes 60-90 minutes (normal)
- Replit will automatically create the correct certificate
- You'll get an email when it's ready

## What Happens Next
- Your website will show "Secure" with a green lock
- No more browser warnings
- Professional appearance restored

## Important Notes
- Don't skip the 15-minute wait in Step 2
- Don't worry if it takes up to 90 minutes after Step 3
- This process is automatic - Replit does all the technical work

## If It Still Doesn't Work
Contact Replit support and say:
"My SSL certificate for selamcpa.com shows the wrong domain name. Please regenerate it."
