# Job Post Builder

Three deliverables in one: the post, the interview kit, and the comp range.

## Inputs to confirm
- Role title and key responsibilities
- Full-time / part-time / contract
- Remote / hybrid / onsite (location if applicable)
- Reports to / team size
- Budget range (or ask me to recommend)
- Years of experience expected

## Steps
1. **Job post** (3 versions):
   - Long version: full responsibilities, requirements, nice-to-haves, what success looks like at 30/60/90 days
   - Short version: LinkedIn-ready, 200-250 words
   - One-liner: for referrals / Twitter
2. **Interview kit**:
   - 6-8 screening questions (15-min phone screen)
   - 4-6 behavioral questions (STAR format)
   - 2-3 technical / scenario questions specific to role
   - Red flags to watch for
3. **Comp envelope**:
   - Market range based on role + location + experience (note: use general knowledge; recommend Glassdoor/Levels verification)
   - Suggested offer range (floor / target / ceiling)
   - Total comp components (base, bonus, equity if any, benefits stipend)
   - Negotiation guidance

## Output
- 3 job post versions
- Interview question kit
- Comp envelope memo
- Suggested 4-week hiring timeline
