# Oceanview Guest House — Pricing Strategy (ZAR)

Prepared August 3, 2026. Rates are launch recommendations — review after the
first 10 bookings.

## 1. Market position

Direct competition (same street / same suburb, from Booking.com and local
aggregators, August 2026):

| Competitor | Location | Entry rate (double) |
|---|---|---|
| Triple9 Hatfield Guesthouse | 999 Arcadia Street (same street) | from ~R400–450 |
| Arcadia-area guest houses (range) | Arcadia, Pretoria | from ~R454 upward |
| Mid-range hotels (Southern Sun Pretoria etc.) | Arcadia | R1,400+ |

Oceanview's edge over the budget end: character house (wood floors, stained
glass, clawfoot bath), large garden, two kitchens, laundry, and strong visible
security. That justifies a small premium over the cheapest neighbours while
staying clearly below hotels.

## 3. Promotions to switch on at launch

| Promotion | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New Property Deal | 20% off first 3 months or first 30 bookings | Booking.com boosts new listings that run it — fastest way to first reviews |
| Genius discount | 10% for Genius members | Large share of SA bookers are Genius; costs little at these rates |
| Weekly rate | 10% off stays of 7+ nights | Attracts contractors and visiting academics |
| Monthly rate | 30% off stays of 28+ nights | Matches the property's proven long-stay market (students/interns) while beating student-rental vacancy |
| Non-refundable rate | extra 10% off | Locks in revenue; guest pays upfront |

Effective launch price for a Genius guest on the double room:
R700 − 20% − 10% ≈ **R504** — right at the market entry point, by design.
After ~10 reviews (target 8.5+), retire the New Property Deal and let net
rates rise back to the base card.

## 5. Revenue outlook (conservative)

Assumptions: 5 rooms live, average achieved rate R650 after discounts,
15% Booking.com commission.

| Occupancy | Room-nights/mo | Gross/mo | After commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30% (first months) | 45 | R29,250 | R24,860 |
| 50% (after reviews) | 75 | R48,750 | R41,440 |
| 70% (established) | 105 | R68,250 | R58,010 |

Reference point: the property's 2024 student-rental income averaged roughly
R29,000/month gross across all rooms (per 822 Arcadia income statement), with
several vacant months. At 50% Booking.com occupancy on just 5 rooms the
property already exceeds that — and the remaining rooms can stay on the
student model during term as a hybrid.
