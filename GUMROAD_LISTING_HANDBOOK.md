## Full description

**You don't need to be a developer to publish an app in 2026. You need the process.**

AI tools now write the code. What actually stops beginners is everything around it:
three different store registrations, review guidelines that reject half of first
submissions, and a Google Play testing rule that silently adds two weeks to every
first-timer's launch.

This handbook is that process, in order, with nothing skipped:

- **Part 1 — Why now:** the real economics ($124 in fees, 170 subscribers to $10K/year)
- **Part 2 — Choose & design:** how to pick an app small enough to actually finish,
  plus the step-by-step Replit walkthrough (the AI builds the app for you)
- **Part 3 — Register:** Apple, Google Play, and Samsung step by step — and the
  12-testers-for-14-days rule that catches almost everyone
- **Part 4 — Monetize:** subscriptions without writing billing code
- **Part 5 — The plan:** a realistic week-by-week 60-day schedule
- **Part 6 — Launch & grow:** build-in-public, reviews, updates

**Plus 3 fill-in worksheets:**
- ✅ The pre-submission launch checklist (accounts, product, listing, distribution)
- ✅ 12-tester recruitment scripts (DM, social post, and community versions)
- ✅ The store listing template (name, screenshots, keywords, review notes)

Instant download. 10 pages, zero filler. Free lifetime updates to future editions.

*Fees and store policies are accurate as of mid-2026 — always verify current terms in
each developer console.*
