# URGENT: SSL Certificate Fix for selamcpa.com

## Current Status
- Domain: selamcpa.com shows "Not secure" warning
- Issue: Using wrong SSL certificate (replit.app instead of selamcpa.com)
- Required Action: Manual intervention in Replit deployment settings

## IMMEDIATE STEPS TO FIX SSL

### Step 1: Access Your Replit Deployment
1. Go to https://replit.com/
2. Log into your account
3. Find your deployed project
4. Click on the "Deployments" tab

### Step 2: Domain Settings
1. In deployments, look for "Custom Domains" section
2. You should see selamcpa.com listed
3. Click the settings/gear icon next to selamcpa.com
4. Look for SSL certificate status

### Step 3: Force SSL Certificate Regeneration
Option A - Disconnect/Reconnect:
1. Click "Remove" or "Disconnect" next to selamcpa.com
2. Wait 2-3 minutes
3. Click "Add Custom Domain" 
4. Re-enter selamcpa.com
5. Wait 30-60 minutes for SSL provisioning

Option B - SSL Regeneration (if available):
1. Look for "Regenerate SSL" or "Request Certificate" button
2. Click it to force new certificate generation
3. Wait 30-60 minutes

### Step 4: Verify DNS Records (Double Check)
Ensure these DNS records are correct in your domain registrar:
- A Record: @ points to Replit's IP address
- CNAME Record: www points to your Replit deployment URL

### Step 5: Wait and Test
1. Wait 30-60 minutes after reconnecting domain
2. Test https://selamcpa.com in incognito mode
3. Certificate should show "CN=selamcpa.com" instead of "CN=replit.app"

## Why This Happens
Replit sometimes serves the default wildcard certificate (*.replit.app) instead of generating a custom certificate for your domain. The domain disconnection/reconnection forces Replit to request a new certificate specifically for selamcpa.com.

## Alternative: Contact Replit Support
If the above steps don't work within 2 hours:
1. Go to Replit support/help
2. Submit ticket: "Custom domain SSL certificate not generating for selamcpa.com"
3. Include: Domain name, deployment ID, and screenshot of SSL error

- Domain reconnection: Immediate
- SSL certificate request: 15-30 minutes
- Certificate deployment: 30-60 minutes
