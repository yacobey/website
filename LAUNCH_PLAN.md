# 1099 Toolkit — Launch Plan

A 14-day plan to get your first sales. Drafts of every post and email are pre-written below — copy, edit, post.

**Prerequisite:** finish the pre-launch checklist at the bottom of `STRIPE_DELIVERY_SETUP.md`. Don't run any of this until the funnel works end-to-end.

## The math you should expect

If you have **~500 LinkedIn followers, ~200 email contacts, and zero ad budget**, this is realistic for week 1:

| Channel | Expected outcomes (week 1) |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn organic posts (5 in week 1) | 2K–8K total impressions, 30–80 page visits, 1–4 sales |
| Email to your existing list | 30–60% open rate, 1–5 sales |
| FB Page posts | Low; minimal conversion without paid boost |
| **Realistic week-1 revenue** | **$160–$700** (2–9 sales × $79 net ~$76 after Stripe fees) |

If you make zero sales in week 1, **don't panic and don't pull the listing.** Sales for new digital products are lumpy — the second sale often comes weeks after the first. The product accumulates trust signals (search results, testimonials, referrals) over months. Keep posting.

## Day 0 — Soft launch (private)

Goal: get 3–5 trusted contacts to buy + feed back honest critique before public launch.

1. Make a list of 5–10 people in your network who:
   - Are self-employed / freelancers themselves
   - Would have honest opinions, not just "looks great"
2. Send each a personal message (DM, text, or 1:1 email). Use this template — **personalize the first line for each person**:

```
Hey [name],

[Personal opener — reference something specific you know about their work.]

I'm launching a small digital product — a $79 tax toolkit for freelancers.
Quarterly tax calculator, expense tracker, mileage log, deductions cheat
sheet. Built it because every January I watch new clients arrive panicked
about a tax bill they didn't plan for.

Before I go public with it, I wanted to ask a favor:

Would you buy it at $40 instead of $79, use it for a week, and tell me
what's missing or confusing? Honest feedback only — I'd rather hear
"this part sucks" than "looks great."

Coupon if you're in: FRIENDS40 (knocks it to $39)
Link: selamcpa.com/1099-toolkit

No pressure either way — and either way, thanks for putting up with me
running this past you.

Yacob
```

3. In Stripe, create the **FRIENDS40** promo code: 50% off, max 10 uses.
4. Wait 48–72 hours for replies. Anyone who buys gives you:
   - Real feedback to refine the product
   - A testimonial (ask for one once they've used it for a week)
   - Social proof for the public launch

### Post 3 — Tactical (Day 7)

```
Three deductions I see freelancers miss every single year:

1. SELF-EMPLOYED HEALTH INSURANCE

2. SOLO 401(k)
```

### Post 4 — Counter-intuitive truth (Day 9)

```
The IRS doesn't penalize you for owing money at tax time.

It penalizes you for not paying along the way.

If you pay either:
- 100% of last year's total tax (110% if AGI > $150K), or
- 90% of this year's actual tax

…through quarterly payments, you can owe $20K in April and the IRS
charges zero penalty. The "safe harbor" rule.

This is the difference between a $20K bill and a $20K bill + $1,800
penalty. Same income. Same year. Just whether you paid along the way.

The Quarterly Calculator in my $79 toolkit defaults to safe harbor.

selamcpa.com/1099-toolkit
```

## Day 7 — Mid-launch check

Open your Make.com `Toolkit Sales` Google Sheet (or Stripe dashboard).

| If you have… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **0 sales** | Look at the LinkedIn post analytics. If impressions are < 500, the issue is reach — keep posting daily. If impressions are 2K+ and 0 clicks to the sales page, your hook is wrong — rewrite post 1 with a different opener. |
| **1–3 sales** | You have product-market fit signal. Keep posting daily. DM each buyer 5 days after they buy and ask for a 2-sentence testimonial. |
| **5+ sales** | You have real demand. Push harder — pay $50 to boost the best-performing LinkedIn post. Cold-email 20 hand-picked freelancers in your network with a personalized note. |

## The boring honest truth about week 1

Most products don't go viral. Most products grow on **steady-state effort over months**. Here's the path you're actually on:

- **Month 1:** $0–$700. You're learning what hooks resonate. Don't change the product.
- **Month 3:** $300–$1,500/mo if you've kept posting weekly. Audience is small but engaged.
- **Month 6:** $800–$3,000/mo. Search traffic starts to compound. Email list of a few hundred.
- **Month 12:** $2K–$6K/mo passive + upsell conversions to your $399 tax prep service that 4–8x the actual revenue per buyer.

The product **only fails** if you stop posting. As long as you keep showing up on LinkedIn 3x/week, the toolkit will accumulate sales.
