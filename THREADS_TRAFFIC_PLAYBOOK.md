## 7-day posting calendar (then repeat the rhythm)

| Day | Post | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The launch thread above | Product clicks |
| 2 | Single value post: the $124 fee breakdown as a standalone (numbers travel well) | Reach |
| 3 | "The 12-testers-for-14-days rule, explained" — end with "full guide in bio" | Authority + clicks |
| 4 | Poll/question: "What's stopped you from building your app idea?" — reply to every answer | Engagement |
| 5 | Mini case study: one screen of the 60-day schedule as an image | Save-worthy content |
| 6 | "3 reasons app store reviewers reject first submissions" | Authority |
| 7 | Recap + soft sell: "This week I broke down how to publish your first app. All of it's in the handbook 👇 [GUMROAD LINK]" | Conversion |

**Cadence rules:**
- 1–2 posts/day; at least two value posts for every sales post.
- Links only in the final post of a thread or in bio — never in the hook.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; early conversation drives ranking.
- Re-run the launch thread with a fresh hook every 2–3 weeks (new angle: cost, the
  trap, the math).
