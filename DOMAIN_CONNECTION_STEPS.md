# Connect selamcpa.com to Current Selam CPA Deployment

## Step-by-Step Domain Connection

### Step 1: Access Your Deployments
1. Go to: https://replit.com/deployments
2. Login to your account
3. You should see your deployments listed

### Step 2: Identify Current Deployment
Look for the deployment that contains your current Selam CPA website:
- The one running your latest website with Selam CPA branding
- Should show "Running" status
- Contains your SEO dashboard and About Us pages

### Step 3: Remove Old Domain Connection
If selamcpa.com is connected to an old deployment:
1. Click on the OLD deployment (likely probalancecpa)
2. Find "Custom Domains" section
3. Remove selamcpa.com from the old deployment
4. Wait 5 minutes for cleanup

### Step 4: Connect to Current Deployment
1. Click on your CURRENT Selam CPA deployment
2. Find "Custom Domains" or "Domains" section
3. Click "Add Custom Domain" or "Connect Domain"
4. Enter: selamcpa.com
5. **IMPORTANT: Enable SSL Certificate**
6. Click "Add" or "Connect"

### Step 5: Verify Connection
After 30-60 minutes:
1. Visit https://selamcpa.com
2. Should show your current Selam CPA website
3. Green SSL lock should appear
4. No more old cached content

## Expected Results
- selamcpa.com shows current Selam CPA branding
- All latest features (SEO dashboard, About Us) visible
- SSL security active
- Professional appearance restored
