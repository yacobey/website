## Post Copy (main weekly post)

Last week's post about federal cleaning contracts struck a nerve — so let's talk about the step where most small businesses quietly give up: **registering on SAM.gov.**

The registration is free. It's also where good businesses stall for weeks. Almost always on the same three screens:

**1️⃣ Entity Validation** — SAM.gov has to match your business name and address to official records *exactly*. If your LLC paperwork says "Street" and you type "St," you can sit in validation limbo for weeks. Fix: copy your name and address character-for-character from your state registration and IRS records before you start.

**2️⃣ UEI & CAGE Code** — You don't apply for these separately (and you never pay anyone for them — they're free). They're generated *through* your SAM.gov registration. If a website is charging you for a UEI, close the tab.

**3️⃣ Reps & Certs** — A wall of legal questions that scares people into quitting at the finish line. Most answers for a small business are straightforward — but guessing wrong here can cause real problems later. This is the screen to get help on, not skip.

Get through those three screens and you're registered — eligible for the micro-purchase work (up to $15,000) we talked about last week, where agencies can simply pick a vendor and pay.
