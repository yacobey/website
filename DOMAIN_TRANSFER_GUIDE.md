# Transfer Current Website to selamcpa.com Domain

## Current Situation
- Your latest website is running on: https://e26ca02e-d597-4e53-a44f-bc802efa5631-00-1hjejlmw99sbo.kirk.replit.dev/
- You want this exact website to be live on: https://selamcpa.com
- The custom domain is currently showing old cached content

## Step-by-Step Domain Transfer

### Step 1: Access Replit Deployments
1. Go to: https://replit.com/deployments
2. Look for your current deployment (the one running your website)
3. Click on the deployment to open its settings

### Step 2: Connect Custom Domain
1. In your deployment, look for "Custom Domains" section
2. If selamcpa.com is already there, **remove it first**
3. Click "Add Custom Domain" or "Connect Domain"
4. Enter: `selamcpa.com`
5. **Make sure SSL certificate is enabled**
6. Click "Add" or "Connect"

### Step 3: Wait for Deployment
- The system will connect your running application to selamcpa.com
- This process takes 15-60 minutes
- Your current website (with SEO dashboard, About Us, etc.) will become live on selamcpa.com

### Step 4: Verify Transfer
1. Wait 30-60 minutes after Step 2
2. Visit https://selamcpa.com
3. You should see your current website with:
   - "Selam CPA" branding
   - SEO dashboard in navigation
   - About Us page
   - All your latest changes

## Important Notes
- Your current development URL will still work
- The custom domain will show the same content as your development URL
- No code changes needed - this is just domain routing
- SSL certificate will be automatically generated for selamcpa.com

## If Domain Connection Fails
1. Check that you own selamcpa.com domain
2. Verify DNS settings point to Replit
3. Contact Replit support if connection doesn't work after 2 hours

## Expected Timeline
- Domain connection: 5-10 minutes
- SSL certificate generation: 30-60 minutes
- Full transfer complete: 60-90 minutes maximum

After completion, selamcpa.com will show exactly the same website as your current development URL.
